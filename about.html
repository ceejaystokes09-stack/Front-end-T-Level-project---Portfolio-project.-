Why Bolton College?

I chose to enroll in Bolton College because of location, compared to my house and getting to and from college it's relatively easy, compared to other colleges. I live in Farnworth so getting to and from college is either going on a local bus taking me straight to the Bolton Interchange in around 25 minutes, or i can walk along Manchester road, which will take around 40 minutes. Another reason i picked Bolton College is due to the course. I REALLY enjoy coding and programming, so when i found out about the T-Level Digital Design and Development and found out about the python and Web development, I was convinced that this coarse was the coarse for me.
